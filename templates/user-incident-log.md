# Project Virgil: Personal Incident Log

> **Instructions:** Print this document or keep it open in a simple text editor on a **clean** device. When in the middle of a cyber incident, it is easy to forget what passwords you changed, what codes you saved, and what times events occurred.

## 2. Containment Checklist
*Check these off as you secure your primary accounts.*

- [ ] **Identity Anchor Secured:** (e.g., Gmail, Apple ID, Outlook).
- [ ] **Master Passwords Rotated:** Changed password manager master key.
- [ ] **Active Sessions Killed:** Logged out of all unrecognized devices.
- [ ] **Financials Locked:** Checked bank accounts / locked credit cards if exposed.
- [ ] **Persistence Cleared:** Checked for hidden email forwarding rules and rogue OAuth apps.
